After a call, here is how to tell whether the agent did a good job.

LangSmith is your call report card and investigation desk. It tells you what our checks found, what saved successfully, where time was spent, and what we could not verify.

Open LangSmith

Sign in to your own workspace and select the project configured for your deployment, commonly servicevoice-live-calls. This public guide omits private workspace and resource addresses; the link above opens the general LangSmith entry page. This is a historical v3 guide: check the review version on a row before applying its instructions.

Release boundary: speech changes require a separate listening acceptance test. Trace and review features described here do not prove that any particular phone assistant has changed or passed that test. Confirm the review version and configuration on the specific call.

Your routine after every call

  1. Finish the call and note the approximate time. The reviewer works afterward, outside the conversation. A short delay before its report appears is normal; there is no guaranteed completion time.
  2. Open the project above and choose the time range covering your call. Look for a row named voice.call.review. One such row is one call review. Its Inputs include the call ID and a private Vapi call link.
  3. Use the v3 filters below. Open the row and examine its Feedback and Outputs. Start with needs_review, then read every failed or uncertain check.
  4. Check the proof. Follow the evidence references and Vapi call link. A saved receipt proves what the database stored; the transcript shows what the provider wrote down. Neither alone proves what someone heard.
  5. Check the reviewer's own health. If your call has no review, inspect the latest voice.review.coverage row. Missing reviews must not silently disappear from your assessment.
The first question is: “Did we have enough evidence?” A green check on one item does not mean the whole call was perfect. A blank score needs explanation.

What are we evaluating?

There are six main questions about the conversation. Separate checks also inspect receipts, confirmation order, evidence completeness and response speed.

LangSmith Feedback nameIn everyday languageExample of something to investigate
hallucination_freeDid it stick to the dealership's facts and the results it actually received?Saying the shop is open on Sunday when the configured hours say it is closed.
booking_accuracyDid the saved appointment match the agreed details?You corrected the vehicle year, but the saved appointment still has the old year.
confirmation_complianceDid it read back the proposal and wait for a later confirmation before saving a change?“Show me a time” being treated as permission to book it.
action_truthfulnessDid it tell the truth about what it had done?“I removed the advisor request” without a successful removal receipt.
task_completionDid it finish the caller's actual goal, or clearly agree on the next step?Ending a booking conversation after collecting details, without a booking or a clear explanation.
conversation_qualityWas the written conversation relevant, clear and responsive to corrections?Repeated questions, pointless loops or ignoring “Actually, make it Thursday.” This score does not judge pronunciation or sound quality.

“No hallucination found” means no unsupported claim was detected in the evidence reviewed. It is not a guarantee that hallucinations are impossible.

How do those grades get produced?

1. Gather the evidenceStored conversation, tool history, dealership facts, saved receipts and timings.
2. Check hard factsCode checks the booking and operation records, confirmation sequence and missing evidence.
3. Read the conversationA Gemini reviewer assesses the six questions using that evidence.
4. Save the reportLangSmith receives scores, safe references and recorded component events.

The automatic reviewer can make mistakes. Its opinion cannot turn a failed booking integrity check into a pass. Missing authoritative booking evidence also prevents a confident booking pass.

The review does not create, cancel or change appointments. Booking safeguards run during the call; the review helps you discover problems afterward.

Quality scores

1 = pass
0 = fail
Blank = uncertain or not applicable

Read the verdict and its reason to distinguish those two blank cases.

Attention flag

needs_review works differently: 1 means investigate. A failed or uncertain check raises it.

0 means the included checks did not request review, not that every audio or vendor detail was observed.

Speed numbers

Latency is in milliseconds: 1,000 ms is one second. Lower is faster.

A missing timing is unknown. It must not be interpreted as an instant response.

Two additional indicators help you avoid trusting a partial report: semantic_assessment_available says the conversation reviewer ran; six_dimensions_scored says no main dimension was uncertain. These describe review coverage, not caller satisfaction. A not-applicable verdict still has no numeric quality score.

LangSmith's “Error” column is not the complete report card. An upload can succeed while the conversation fails a quality check. Look at Feedback and needs_review, not just whether the trace has a red technical error.

Open one call and follow the evidence

  1. In Feedback, select the failed or uncertain check and read its fixed reason and evidence references.
  2. In Outputs → evidence_index, find that reference. For example, turn:12 identifies a stored message and its offset from the start; tool:3 identifies a tool event. These are review references, not guaranteed labels on the provider's screen.
  3. Use Inputs → vapi_call_url to inspect the source call in Vapi. If a recording exists and you have access, listen around that offset. If recording was off or unavailable, leave the question of exactly what was spoken unresolved.
  4. For a booking, inspect Outputs → booking and the authoritative receipt evidence. Customer names, numbers, transcript text and raw tool arguments are deliberately excluded from this LangSmith export; detailed records remain in the protected source systems.

You can add your own review using the trace's … → Notes menu. Record the timestamp, what you observed and whether it disagrees with the automatic grade. Keep personal data out of notes; do not overwrite an automatic judgment as though it never happened.

The automated review sends bounded conversation evidence to Gemini for judging. The LangSmith export contains safe checks and references rather than the raw conversation.

The detailed trace is the call's trail of name tags

Audio path

Your microphone → voice service and speech recognition → synthesized speech → your speaker.

We observe available provider events and our speech adapter. Your device and hidden vendor internals can remain unknown.

Decision path

Recognized words → gateway → narrow FAQ route or Gemini → approved response.

Look here for thinking delays, fallback decisions, invalid requests or unsupported claims.

Business path

Proposed action → guarded tool → scheduler → saved receipt.

Look here for rejected changes, time conflicts, confirmation checks and uncertain operations.

Inside a call review, expand the recorded child rows. voice.turn.observed_events groups an exchange, voice.request.observed_events groups one request, and voice.operation.observed_events groups evidence for an action. Rows starting voice.component. show actual recorded boundaries.

Configuration means which local source and settings were captured. Call is the whole conversation. Turn identifies a conversation step. Operation links an action to its receipt. Request IDs connect related server work.

In child-row metadata, inspect configuration_id, turn_id and operation_id when present. Under Outputs, inspect the outcome and safe measurements. Groups show a time envelope of related events; they are not separate measured execution stages and their times must not be added together.

In Outputs → diagnostics → components, each component answers five questions: what enters, what leaves, who owns it, what failure looks like, and how it is observed. Under diagnostics.coverage and trace_export, check for losses, skipped events or absent evidence. “Observed” means some evidence was captured, not complete visibility. Local configuration hashes do not independently prove the exact settings published at a provider.

Make sure the reviewer itself is working

In the newest voice.review.coverage row, open Outputs. Read these before quoting a success rate:

  • Known ended calls / completed reviews: how many known finished calls should have a review, and how many do.
  • Semantically assessed / fully scored: how many received a conversation assessment, and how many were assessed without uncertainty. A completed upload may still contain unknown grades.
  • Oldest pending review age / review lag: whether unfinished reviews are piling up and how late completed reports arrived.
  • Unavailable records / discovery errors: whether the provider record could not be fetched or the call list may be incomplete. Unknown or unavailable calls are never successful calls by default.
  • Monthly model attempts / deadletters / alerts: whether the judging allowance is exhausted, a report failed repeatedly, or operator attention is required.
Alerts here are status records. They do not automatically send you an email, text or Slack message. A separate notification destination would be needed for that.

For example, if ten calls ended but only six have reviews, “all six passed” is not a complete picture of the ten calls. Review coverage and agent quality are different numbers. Also exclude the coverage rows and component child rows when counting calls.

How to make your own final judgment

For each important call, ask: Did we finish the right task, save only an approved change, describe it truthfully, and respond quickly enough? Then ask whether you had enough evidence to answer.

Investigate every failed or uncertain booking/confirmation check. Listen to a sample of apparently good calls too, when recordings are available. Include corrections, interruptions, different languages and digits in controlled listening tests. Turn confirmed failures into safe fictional regression examples.

No report alone certifies the whole system as production grade. This setup makes the evidence, missing coverage and reviewer failures visible; human listening and independent checks remain part of supervision.
